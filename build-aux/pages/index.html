Cafe

The right amount of coffee, every brew.

Pick a brewing method and enter either the coffee or the water. Cafe fills in the other, in grams and millilitres or ounces and fluid ounces.

Install for GNOME
flatpak install --user @URL@/cafe.flatpakref

Updates arrive with flatpak update and through GNOME Software.

Needs the GNOME 50 runtime, which Flatpak installs from Flathub.
